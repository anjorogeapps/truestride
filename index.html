Truestride

Walking recovery after knee replacement surgery.

After a knee replacement, the hardest question isn't the pain. It's whether what you're feeling is normal.

Your iPhone has quietly been measuring how you walk this whole time — speed, step length, how evenly you're using each leg. Truestride shows you what it recorded, and what those numbers usually look like at your point in recovery, based on a published study of 686 total knee replacement patients.

No account. No server. Nothing about you leaves your phone.
